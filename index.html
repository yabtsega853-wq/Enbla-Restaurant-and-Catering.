01Our Story

A family table.
An Ethiopian welcome.

Enbla began in 2018 with a simple idea — that a meal should feel like coming home, even if you've never been here before.

We are a family restaurant in Summit, around Fiyel Bet. We cook the food we grew up with: kitfo, kurte, tebesatebes, slow-cooked beef fried rice. We serve it the way our families served it — generously, and without hurry.

Whether you are dining in, taking away, or planning a celebration for two hundred, we bring the same fire and the same care to every plate.
