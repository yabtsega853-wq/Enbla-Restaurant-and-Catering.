01 Our Story

More than a meal.
An experience.

EMBER was born from a single idea — that fire, patience, and craft can turn a meal into a memory.

In 2018, we opened a small room on a quiet Mayfair street with ten tables and one wood-fired oven. Every plate that left the pass carried the same conviction: that the best food is honest, seasonal, and served with intention.

Today the room is still intimate, the menu still changes with the seasons, and every evening still ends the same way — with a table of guests who don't want to leave.
